Frequently Asked Questions

Helpful answers before you book your Bellevue spa appointment.

How much do facials cost at Natural Beauty Spa?

Facial treatments at Natural Beauty Spa typically start at $128 for Deep Cleansing Facial, Classic Hydrating Facial, and Diamond Microdermabrasion. Specialty treatments include Japanese Cypress Lifting Facial at $168, Chinese Guasha Lifting Facial at $198, and Cellcosmet Anti-Aging Facial at $268. For the full Bellevue spa pricing menu, visit our pricing page.

Do I need an appointment, or do you take walk-ins?

Appointments are strongly recommended to guarantee your preferred time and treatment. Walk-ins may be available depending on the schedule, but evenings and weekends can fill quickly.

What’s the difference between Signature, Deluxe, and Platinum HydraFacial?

Signature is the foundational HydraFacial. Deluxe adds targeted booster serums and LED light therapy. Platinum includes the full treatment experience with added lymphatic drainage and premium serums.

How long do facial results last?

Many guests notice an immediate glow after one visit, with results that build through consistent treatments. HydraFacial is commonly booked monthly for best long-term skin care maintenance.

Are your estheticians licensed and trained?

Yes. Treatments are performed by trained spa professionals, and advanced treatments are handled by staff trained for those services. We do not place untrained staff on advanced skin care treatments.

Do you offer gift cards?

Yes. Gift cards are available and are popular for birthdays, anniversaries, Mother’s Day, holidays, and special occasions.
